Satoshi Nakomoto (author of bitcoin)
Peer-to-peer electronic cash system

## Traditional payment model problems
* brick and mortar
* transactions may be recessed
* mediation increases transaction costs
* transaction costs make small payments economically impractical
* Merchant must accept payment uncertainty and some risk of fraud


## Double-spending
* A transaction can be authorized using a private key
* The corresponding public key can be used to verify the signature
* modifying the signed transactions invalidates the signatures
* Valid signature != valid payment

## Nakamoto's solution
* Pere-to-peer network
* publicly announced transactions
* History will be too large to fake
* Assumes honest nodes have more coverage
